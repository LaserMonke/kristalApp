Stock Options Academy is an educational app that teaches how stock options and
derivatives work. It is operated by «LEGAL_ENTITY».

This policy explains what we collect, why, who else sees it, and what you can
do about it. We have tried to write it in plain language rather than the usual
wall of legal text.

**The short version:** we ask for a username, a password and your rough
education level. We do not ask for your email, your real name, your date of
birth, or your phone number. We do not run ads, and we do not track you across
other apps or websites. Your username and your point total are visible to other
learners on the leaderboard. Nothing else about you is.

---

## 1. What we collect

**Things you give us when you sign up**

| What | Why | Where it is stored |
|---|---|---|
| Username (chosen by you) | Identifies your account and appears on the leaderboard | Our server and your device |
| Password | Signing you in | Our server, hashed — never stored or transmitted in a readable form |
| Education level (a coarse band, e.g. "undergraduate") | Pitches lesson difficulty and the learning path at roughly the right level | Our server and your device |

**Things the app records as you learn**

- Which lessons you have finished
- Your answers' scores on the Q&A after each lesson (how many correct, out of how many)
- Your points total, your level, and your daily streak

**Things we do NOT collect**

- No email address
- No real name, date of birth, or age
- No phone number or contacts
- No location
- No advertising identifiers, and no cross-app or cross-site tracking
- No analytics SDK, no ad network, no social-media SDK

### A note on the "email" attached to your account

Our sign-in system is built on a service that expects every account to have an
email address. Because we deliberately do not ask you for one, the app
generates a fake, unroutable placeholder from your username — of the form
`yourname@users.optionsschool.invalid`. `.invalid` is a reserved suffix that
cannot exist on the real internet. No mail is ever sent to it, it reaches no
mailbox, and you will never see or type it.

**A consequence you should know before signing up:** because we hold no real
email address for you, there is no "forgot password" link and no way for us to
verify who you are. If you lose your password, that account cannot be
recovered. Keep it somewhere safe.

## 2. What other people can see

On the **leaderboard**, other learners can see:

- your username
- your points total

That is all. Other learners cannot see your education level, which lessons you
have done, what you got wrong, or your streak.

The leaderboard may also contain **bot entries** — scores generated by us, not
real people. These are always labelled as bots in the app. We will never
present a bot's score as a real person's.

If you would rather not appear on the leaderboard at all, see "Your choices"
below.

---

## 3. Where your data goes

**Our server.** Your account and progress are stored on infrastructure provided
by **Supabase** (database, authentication and hosting), acting as our data
processor. This is what lets your progress follow you to a new device.

**Your device.** A copy of your progress is also kept locally so the app works
offline and syncs when you are back online. Your app settings (theme, whether
reminders are on) stay on your device only.

**Market data.** The practice market shows delayed share prices, supplied by
**Finnhub**. Requests go through our own server, which sends Finnhub only the
ticker symbol being looked up. Your username, your account, and anything about
your learning are **never** sent to Finnhub, and Finnhub is not told who is
asking.

**Payments.** If you choose to unlock the practice market, the purchase is
handled by **Google Play**, under Google's own privacy policy. We never see or
store your card details. <!-- Add RevenueCat here once the store layer ships —
it is a third-party processor and must be named. -->

We do not sell your data. We do not share it with advertisers. We have no
interest in doing either.

## 4. Notifications

If you turn on the daily learning reminder, the app schedules it **on your
device**. The reminder is not sent from our server, and we do not learn whether
you opened it. It is off-switchable at any time in Settings, and you can also
revoke the notification permission in your device settings.

## 6. How long we keep things

We keep your account and progress for as long as your account exists. If you
ask us to delete it, we remove it within 30 days, apart from anything we are
required to retain by law. Backups are overwritten on a rolling basis.

## 7. Age

This app is rated **16+** and is not directed at children.

We ask for a coarse education level, but we do not ask for your age or date of
birth, and we cannot verify it. If you are under 16 (or under the age of
consent where you live, which is between 13 and 16 across the EU), please do
not create an account without a parent or guardian's involvement. If you
believe someone under that age has created an account, contact
«CONTACT_EMAIL» and we will remove it.

Options trading is not a suitable pursuit for children, and this app does not
promote it to anyone. It teaches how the instruments work — including how they
lose money — and it never enables real trading with real money.

## 8. Security

Passwords are hashed by our authentication provider and are never stored in a
readable form, including by us. Data in transit is encrypted (HTTPS).
Database-level access rules mean each account can only read and write its own
rows; the only exception is the leaderboard, which exposes nothing but a
username and a point total.

No system is perfectly secure, and we will not pretend otherwise. Because we
hold so little about you — no email, no real name, no payment details, no
location — the damage a breach could do is deliberately limited.

---

## 9. This is an educational app, not a financial service

Stock Options Academy is educational only. Nothing in it is financial,
investment, tax or legal advice, or a recommendation to buy or sell any
security. The practice market uses simulated money and idealised models; no
real trade is ever placed and no real money is ever at risk.
